Wellness Recommendations

Small consistent improvements in daily routine can significantly improve mental well-being and emotional balance.

Daily Mental Wellness Tips

  • Maintain a regular sleep schedule (7–8 hours)
  • Take short breaks during study or work sessions
  • Avoid comparing your progress with others
  • Talk openly with trusted friends, mentors, or family
  • Reduce excessive screen time before sleeping
  • Practice simple breathing exercises during stress
  • Follow a balanced diet and stay hydrated

When to Seek Extra Support

If stress, sadness, anxiety, or emotional exhaustion continues for a long period and starts affecting daily life, it is important to seek support from a counselor, mentor, or mental health professional.

Suggested Support Options

  • College Counselor or Faculty Mentor
  • Trusted Friend or Family Member
  • Professional Mental Health Counselor
  • Career Guidance Mentor for placement stress
